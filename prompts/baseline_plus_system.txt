Notes:
- Each service stamps logs with its own clock. Clocks of different services can be offset from each other by up to several seconds, so do not trust cross-service ordering of nearby timestamps.

- A symptom that appears in one service is often caused by another service.

General principles:
- Background noise exists: a few isolated errors, slow requests and warnings occur even in healthy periods. Judge a cause by the sustained burst of failures, not by the first failed request.
- Timeouts, queueing and load shedding are usually effects. Prefer an explanation that shows a trigger (a deploy or config change, a drop in a service's worker limit, a crash or restart, a pool running out, a service's own slow processing) over one that only shows downstream consequences.
