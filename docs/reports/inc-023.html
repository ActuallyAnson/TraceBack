Alternatives considered

  • capacity_saturation in orders: Orders showed high queue depth and pinned workers only after payments slowed, indicating it was an effect of waiting on payments, not a capacity loss.
  • capacity_saturation in payments: Payments also showed queued requests but its workers were not reduced; the queue built up because calls timed out and retried, not due to lost capacity.
  • edge_unreachable in orders: Gateway timeout traces had corresponding orders client charge spans (e.g., orders-S00a99), proving calls reached orders; thus not edge unreachable.
  • internal_errors in gateway: Internal errors in gateway occurred only as isolated early spikes before the sustained burst and did not correlate with the failure wave.
