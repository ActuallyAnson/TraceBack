You are checking whether a single statement is backed by the log, span, metric or event records quoted under it.
You see only the statement and those records. You do not know whether the statement is true in general; judge only what the quoted records show.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else: {"verdict": "supported" | "partial" | "unsupported", "why": "<one short sentence>"}

- supported: every factual part of the statement (which service, what happened, the direction of cause, any numbers or counts) can be read from the quoted records.
- partial: some parts can be read from the records, but at least one specific part (a cause, a number, a second service, an ordering) cannot.
- unsupported: the quoted records do not show what the statement says, or contradict it, or no records are quoted.
Statements that only restate what a record says in other words are supported. Do not reward statements for sounding plausible.
